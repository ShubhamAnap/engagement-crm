# India GTM playbook — Engage CRM

Phase 1 of the public SaaS plan: win **India** with focused niches before worldwide marketing.

## Locked niches (pick depth in this order)

1. **UPS / energy / industrial dealers** — WhatsApp quotes, shared inbox, catalogue links  
2. **Clinics & local service businesses** — missed calls / WhatsApp booking follow-up  
3. *(Optional third)* Coaching / tuition centers — parent updates via WhatsApp  

Do not chase every vertical in month 1. Two niches max until 10 paying orgs.

## Ideal buyer

- Team of **3–50** people who already use WhatsApp Business for customers  
- Pain: messages stuck on one phone; no shared history; no simple AI FAQ  
- Can pay **₹2,999–₹9,999/mo** (Starter / Pro) or start on Free and upgrade  

## 90-day sales rhythm

| Window | Activity | Goal |
|--------|----------|------|
| Days 1–14 | Pricing + legal live; Razorpay on; Loom demo; support inbox monitored | Launch packaging done |
| Days 15–45 | **50 outbound conversations / week** (LinkedIn + WhatsApp + warm intros) | 10 trials, **5 paid** |
| Days 46–90 | Fix onboarding friction; one case study; partner with 1 agency | **10+ paying orgs** |

### Weekly founder checklist

- [ ] 50 new conversations (or 10 demos) logged in the tracker below  
- [ ] All trials invited to connect WhatsApp within 48h  
- [ ] Support inbox cleared within SLA (`/support`)  
- [ ] `/platform` Risk skimmed for spend spikes  

## Outreach script (short)

> We built Engage CRM — shared WhatsApp + AI inbox for teams like yours.  
> Free workspace in 5 minutes: {APP_URL}/signup  
> Pricing: {APP_URL}/pricing  
> If useful, I can walk you through a 15-min demo this week.

## Demo outline (3–15 min)

1. Signup → workspace name  
2. Channels → connect WhatsApp (or show widget)  
3. Inbox → AI draft + human takeover  
4. Knowledge → one FAQ doc  
5. Billing → Free caps → upgrade path  
6. Ask: seats, monthly WhatsApp volume, decision date  

Record a Loom and set `VITE_DEMO_VIDEO_URL` on Render so `/features` and `/pricing` link it.
