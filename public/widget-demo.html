Sample customer website

This page simulates any customer site. The EnerTech chat bubble is loaded via widget.js. Messages appear in EnerTech Engage → Inbox.

1. Open Channels in EnerTech Engage and copy the embed snippet.
2. Paste it here (or set data-key below from your .env VITE_WIDGET_PUBLIC_KEY).
3. Refresh this page, open the bubble, send a message, then check Inbox.
